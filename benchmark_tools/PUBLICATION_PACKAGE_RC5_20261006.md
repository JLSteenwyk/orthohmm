## Scope And Limits

QfO and OrthoBench are development-exposed primary benchmarks; Three Kingdoms
is supplementary. New manuscript tables come from already-reviewed summaries,
not new scores or bootstrap draws. Group-connection paths are software
localization, not proof of biological false-homology mechanisms. SwissTrees
adjusted F1 intervals include zero; a six-endpoint mean is not official QfO F1.

Shared Threadripper measurements may have unknown, tool-dependent CPU,
memory-bandwidth and I/O contention. They do not estimate isolated performance.
No DGX, quiet host or interference with unrelated analyses is required.

Full native factorial completion, independent-family confirmation, several QfO
uncertainty endpoints, original TreeFam family reconstruction, complete raw and
dependency closure, all-method native restoration and rights clearance remain
unfinished. This is not a hermetic full-study archive, publication-ready
release, deposited dataset, archival DOI or journal submission. Versioned
selection/build/archive/restoration/execution receipts govern what actually ran;
this guide alone is not proof of execution.
