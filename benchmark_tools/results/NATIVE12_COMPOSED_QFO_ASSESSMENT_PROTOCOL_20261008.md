## Scientific Contract

Use the unchanged `command_for`, frozen QfO 2020 reference and original
GO, EC, VGNC, SwissTrees, TreeFam-A and FAS endpoints. The worker records its
source, exact execution spec, prediction references, interval, log and complete
output inventory, and rechecks all source/input records after execution.
A successful process is pending independent admission, not an admitted score.

Independent admission requires actual successful 8-CPU / 128-GiB producer
accounting, exact preflight/spec/source equality, unchanged full output inventory,
one complete native trace, original endpoint validator and original FAS sample
audit. Mutated predictions, native row counts, participant IDs, missing endpoints,
failed tasks, missing FAS scores or changed output inventory reject admission.
Failures retain their evidence and do not authorize retries or imputation.

VGNC, SwissTrees and TreeFam-A F1 are distinct from GO/EC similarities and FAS.
The six-metric mean is secondary and project-defined. QfO remains development-
exposed; native SEMs and unseeded FAS sampling do not supply independent-family
or paired-method confidence intervals. No original-schema, isolated timing,
independent-test or publication-readiness claim is made by this workflow.

The assessment batch arguments are pairs-manifest path/digest, actual conversion
job, and worker-source digest. Admission adds the actual assessment job before
its worker-source digest. Submit each once only when successful validated
prerequisites exist, inspect the owned held envelope and source bindings,
commit/push, capacity-check and release once. No production native12 scoring
or admission is submitted by implementation or tests.
