Implement the user's [prospective amendment](PUBLICATION_SHARED_HOST_AMENDMENT_20261003.md)
as an explicit request, readiness and environmental scope, not by removing the

## Implemented Route

- Private deployment plus an externally pinned current runtime lookup and
  execution scope `shared_host_matched_resources` select the new scheduler
  entry point. It requests 64 task slots, 128 GiB and 26 hours without node-wide
  exclusivity; native commands still use CPUs 0-31 and 32 threads.
- Shared readiness requires validated observer accounting, frozen policy and
  contention annotation, explicitly not isolation. Historical full-scale
  quiet-host overhead certification is not required for this descriptive panel.
- Environment/process v3 policies annotate foreign CPU activity, process churn
  and PSI. No ordinary-process/image/service whitelist certifies unrelated work.
  Preflight does not claim absence of scientific work or uncontended timing.
- Preflight checks at least 128 GiB available memory before and after its short
  observation, allowing the complete frozen native limit at launch. This does
  not guarantee future host capacity; native limits and failures remain recorded.
- Same-boot observer/job identity, ordered valid counters, in-job attribution,
  complete sample cadence/bracketing, pressure evidence, collector state,
  source/runtime/input hashes and release deadlines remain enforced.
- Outside sampling errors and unmatched/changing processes limit contention
  estimates and are retained. Missing/invalid observer evidence or membership
  changes are not converted into annotation-only passes.
- Post-run review recomputes every interval. Accepted shared observations remain
  potentially confounded and never authorize the next identity automatically.
  All existing historical modes retain their stricter decisions.
