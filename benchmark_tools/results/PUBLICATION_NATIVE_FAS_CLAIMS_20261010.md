## Scope Of Delivery

This is integration of retained outcomes into the requested claim checklist,
not a new statistical estimator, inference, native rescore, bootstrap draw,
coverage experiment, manuscript revision or release-candidate gate. The
existing review PDF renders the unchanged current manuscript. This checklist
does not silently fulfill an unmet scientific requirement by recording its
unavailability. Historical checklists retain their own evidence dates.

Scoped verification actually completed: Pandoc structural parsing finds eight
three-column claim rows. All 25 local link/image occurrences resolve to 23
tracked targets whose present hashes were checked. The quoted intervals and
observed reconciliation difference match the retained full-precision results;
eight/28 and four/six inventories, four newer zero-including contrasts, false
biological/readiness flags, historical binding limits and preserved failed
timing were checked. This readback changes no intervals and does not prove
their scientific assumptions or full-goal completion.
